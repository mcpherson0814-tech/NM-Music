About

Nathan McPherson

Nathan McPherson  ·  2025

Nathan McPherson is an indie country and Americana songwriter whose music lives in the dust and wide light of the American Southwest. His songs are built on honest storytelling, fingerpicked melodies, and a quiet conviction that the best moments often go unannounced.

Drawing on influences that range from classic country to folk and beyond, Nathan writes music that's rooted but restless — the kind of songs that feel at home on a long drive with no particular destination.

Better Never Than Late, his debut release, is out now.
