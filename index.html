The Music

Album  ·  2026

Better Never
Than Late

Better Never Than Late is a record about time: how it moves, what it takes, what it gives, and what it leaves behind. Across the album, Nathan McPherson writes about the journey of family, making peace with loss, and about ordinary people caught under forces bigger than themselves. The songs feel unhurried and honest, somewhere between classic country and something entirely his own. Produced by Nick Costa (nick-costa.com). Nick's masterful touch and vision can be heard throughout each note. His musical versatility and layering shines beautifully through the album.

About

Nathan McPherson

Nathan McPherson  ·  2026

Nathan McPherson writes Indie Country and Americana: honest songs that don't chase any single sound. He grew up in Minnesota and has lived in Wisconsin for the last decade plus.

He's spent years building things, in business and in music, and after a long stretch away from writing, he's come back to what he loves to do. Some of these songs are brand new. Others, he started a long time ago and only recently recorded.

Off the clock, you'll find him hunting, fishing, or home with his wife, their two kids, and their dogs.

Better Never Than Late, his debut, is coming soon.
